# 02e. Hands-on playtest (iPad apps on a Mac)

Date: 22 Nov 2026. Machine: Apple silicon Mac mini. Both games ran as iPad apps on the Mac, installed by the owner.
Purpose: fill the gaps left by store-page research in `02b` and `02c`. Research only. We reuse no names, art, text or levels from these games.

Screenshots: `screens/played/castle-busters/` (20) and `screens/played/pocket-tanks/` (14). All under 300 KB.

---

## 1. What we did

| Game | Time | Matches | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Castle Busters (the "Castle Clashers" game) | about 30 min in two blocks | 6 battles: 2 tutorial (owner was also giving input), 4 driven by us | 4 wins, 2 losses (one by timeout, one at the round cap) |
| Pocket Tanks | about 28 min | 4 matches vs CPU level 5, 5, 2, 2 | 4 losses (one by 1 point, 202 to 203) |

We never bought anything, never watched an ad, never signed in. Game Center was already signed in to the owner's account.

What we touched in Castle Busters: battle (aim, unit pick, castle drive), home, Castle (Building and Upgrading tabs), Units (stats, equip, room placement), Shop (Offers, Currency, Market tabs, view only), Forge Pass, Treasure Hunt leaderboard, daily chests. Utilities (gadgets) stayed locked.

What we touched in Pocket Tanks: title, Options, mode menu, CPU difficulty, weapon draft (manual and Quick Pick), every HUD control (Move, Weapon, Angle, Power, Fire), wind on and off.

Note on the first 10 minutes of Castle Busters: battles started and turns fired without our input. The owner confirmed they were also playing. Our own clicks were not landing at the time (see section 4). Every number below comes from turns we drove ourselves unless we say so.

## 2. Castle Busters

### 2.1 Turn flow
1. Tap Battle. A loading tip shows ("Aim low. Break the base and let gravity finish the job."). "Searching" then "Opponent Found" with both castles and unit cards.
2. Both castles roll in on tracks over **hilly** ground. The ground is not flat. Castles tilt on slopes.
3. Turns alternate. The round counter counts every shot by both sides: "ROUND 2/14" is your first shot if the foe went first.
4. On your turn the camera cuts to your castle as a cutaway. You see your units on ledges, a fuel bar, left and right drive arrows and a row of unit cards.
5. Tap a unit card to pick the shooter. Then press and drag back anywhere on screen: the selected unit aims. Power (%) and angle (degrees) show at the top, with a short dotted aim line and a cancel X in the middle of the screen. Release to fire.
6. The camera follows the shot, then cuts to the foe's castle for their turn.
7. Round cap: **12 rounds in Arena 1, 14 in Arena 2.** A "3 rounds remain" banner warns near the end.
8. At the cap, both castles shrink to silhouettes with their health % side by side. Higher % wins.
9. Result screen: "Battle Successful" or "Battle Failed", rewards, an MVP card (castle damage %, units destroyed), a purple watch-ad button for double rewards, and a plain Continue below it.

### 2.2 Timing
- Turn clock: **30 seconds**, shown as a bar under the round counter (blue on your turn, red on theirs).
- The foe's turn takes about 8 to 12 seconds including the shot.
- One battle of 14 rounds took about 4 to 5 minutes.

### 2.3 Controls feel (mouse standing in for touch)
- Pull-back aim felt natural and readable. Power and angle numbers made it easy to repeat a good shot.
- The aim line is short. You still have to judge most of the arc by eye.
- Picking a unit by card is clear. A tap on the wrong card fires the wrong unit, which matches one store review.
- Driving: holding the right arrow for 1.5 seconds drained almost the whole fuel bar. Fuel looks per turn.

### 2.4 Castle and unit behavior
- Castle is a grid of blocks on a wooden base with two track sets. Hits punch holes. Units on exposed ledges take direct hits.
- A dead unit leaves a "RIP" stone on its ledge. Its card shows an X on the HUD.
- Units seen: Wrecker (one big bomb), Bomber (4 bombs), a skeleton unit (rocket volley). Stats screen for each unit:

| Unit | Health | Unit damage | Accuracy | Explosion | Projectiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wrecker, common, lvl 1 | 120 | 70 | 85% | 5.62 m | 1 |
| Bomber, common, lvl 1 | 100 (+6 next) | 50 (+3) | 70% | 3.73 m (+0.08) | 4 |

- Squad slots open at Arenas 3, 5, 7, 11 and 16. Units are dragged onto ledges inside the castle before battle.
- Builders (who speed block upgrades) unlock at Arenas 3, 8 and 16.
- Floating bonus rings ("+4", "x2") drift near the castles. A crate with a fire icon also floated in the sky. We did not hit one, so we did not see the effect.
- Low shots at the base did the most damage. Shots at roofs did little.

### 2.5 Economy and timers met
- Win: +29 or +30 trophies, 30 to 45 gold, 10 to 15 wood.
- Loss: -12 trophies, and still 45 gold and 15 wood. A loss pays close to a win in resources.
- Daily chests: one fills per win, **5 per day**. After 5, the next Battle shows "Don't miss out! Without resetting, you won't earn new chests when you win!" with an ad button to reset.
- A chest gave 40 gold and 4 cards each for three units.
- Castle Level 1: 4 of 7 blocks at first. New block slot: 50 wood. Block upgrade wood to stone: 75 gold + 25 wood, 3 second timer. Next tier: 300 gold + 100 wood. Castle Level rises only at Forge Level 14.
- First unit level-up: 2 second timer, or 50 gems to skip.
- Forge Pass: free row and paid row (paid row pays about 1.5x). Paid pass $14.99 for 14 days.

### 2.6 Shop and ads met
- Gems: 10 free per ad (3 a day), 80 for $0.99, 500 for $4.99, 1,200 for $9.99, 2,500 for $19.99, 6,500 for $49.99, 14,000 for $99.99.
- Special offer: 4 unit card packs plus 5,000 gold, 1,000 wood, 500 gems for $2.99, tagged "1000% value".
- Market: chests for 200, 750 and 2,000 gems. Emotes for 150 gems each.
- The watch-ad button shows after every battle result. We saw no forced interstitial ads in about 30 minutes.
- After the timeout loss, a macOS App Store system window opened over the game. We did not touch it and left it for the owner. We do not know what it asked for.
- Weekend "Treasure Hunt" leaderboard: map pieces per win, 20 reward steps, a 100 gem grand prize.

### 2.7 Fun moments
- Bombs landing low and a wall caving in to show a unit inside.
- Killing a unit and seeing its card go grey with an X.
- Close finishes at the cap: 42% to 38% (win), 57% to 56% with two rounds left (then a loss).

### 2.8 Pain points
- Clock pressure plus a slow camera cut means little time to think on your turn.
- The aim line is too short to plan bank shots.
- Results screens push the ad button first. Continue is small text under it.
- Daily chest cap and the ad to reset it feel like a wall after 5 wins.
- Gadgets stay locked for the whole early game, so the HUD is one note.

## 3. Pocket Tanks

### 3.1 Turn flow
1. Play, then One Player, then name and tank color, then CPU difficulty (1 to 7).
2. Weapon draft ("Weapon Shop"): 20 weapons on offer. Players pick in turns, one each, until each has 10. Quick Pick drafts the rest for you.
3. "begin game". 10 volleys. Each volley you fire one weapon, then the CPU fires. Weapons are used in list order unless you pick another.
4. Points come from damage. The higher score after the final volley wins. Then "Play again? Yes / No".

### 3.2 Timing
- **No turn clock.** We sat on our turn for over 12 seconds with no prompt.
- One match took about 6 to 7 minutes.

### 3.3 Controls feel
- HUD at the bottom: Move (count), Weapon, Fire, Angle, Power.
- Tap Power: a slider panel with -1 and +1 arrows and a check. Tap Angle: a ruler panel with the same arrows. Precise and calm, but it takes three taps to change one value.
- The angle arrows ran backward to what we expected: the left arrow raised the angle.
- Move: 4 moves per match. Moving does not end your turn.
- Weapon list: a scroll wheel of what you still hold.
- The camera zooms out for each shot. Where a miss landed was hard to read, so tuning power took several shots.

### 3.4 Terrain and tanks
- Random hills each match. Craters stay. Weapons can bury a tank in dirt, dig a pit under it, or wrap it in barbed wire.
- Hitting yourself costs points: a Quantum Cube cost us 25, a Big Shot into our own pit wall cost 21, wire around our tank cost 1 a tick. Score floored at 0.

### 3.5 Weapons seen
Super Star (bouncing stars, about 2 points each), Dirtball, Crazy Ivan, Chain Reaction, Homing Missile, Hail Storm, 3 Shot, Worm, Mountain Mover (raises a spiral mound), Zapper, Missile Turret (sets a turret on the hill), Tommy Gun, Firecracker, Pineapple, Homing Worm, Scatter Shot, Barbed Wire, Ground Hog, Gamma Blaster, Sniper Rifle (straight line, useless without line of sight), Fireball (burned for about 90 points), Quantum Cube, Skipper, Big Shot, Evac (moves your tank), Oil Slick, Backtrack (flies back toward you), Pile Driver, Cruiser, Drillers, Magic Wall, Dirt Slinger, Tracer, Napalm, Bouncy Blocker, Jackhammer.
The free app has 45 of 400 weapons. The title screen sells weapon packs and a Deluxe version.

### 3.6 Options
Terrain type, explosion size, explosion kick, wind speed (None, Low, ...), wind variability, wind change (Never, Per Shot, ...), a weapon ban list, saved presets, terrain zoom. Wind is **off by default**. With wind on, the HUD shows it under the volley count, for example "Wind <- 12" or "Wind 19 ->".

### 3.7 Fun moments
- The draft. Picking a weapon the CPU wanted is a real choice.
- Super Star and Fireball hits that rack up points.
- Losing by a single point on the last volley.

### 3.8 Pain points
- No aim line at all. Range is pure trial and error, and wind makes it worse.
- Self-damage from dirt and pit weapons the CPU set up felt unfair and hard to escape.
- Even CPU level 2 beat us every time. The CPU dug pits and buried our tank, then hit at will.
- Sniper and Skipper are dead picks on hilly maps.

## 4. Mac port quirks (likely not on a real iPad)
- **A normal mouse click does nothing in either game.** Only a held press (button down for about 0.2 seconds, then up) registers. This explains the first 10 minutes, when our clicks failed and the owner's input drove the game.
- Drags work once the press is held.
- The window keeps an iPad aspect ratio. Window menu "Fill" did not resize it.
- Castle Busters restarted and logged in again after it sat in the background for about 25 minutes.
- If another app takes focus during a drag, the press gets stuck until you bring the game back.
None of these seem to be game design. A phone or tablet test would not show them.

## 5. Answers to the open questions in 02b section 19

1. **Turn clock length:** 30 seconds. If you let it run out, your turn is skipped and your next turn gets a short clock (a few seconds) with "Fire a shot to restore your full timer!". Three missed turns in a row lost the match ("Battle Failed", -12 trophies).
2. **Wind:** none in Castle Busters. No gauge, and the same pull gave the same arc every turn. Pocket Tanks has wind as an option, off by default.
3. **Tie break at the round cap:** the game compares castle health %. Higher % wins. We won 42% to 38% and lost on another cap finish. We did not see an exact tie.
4. **Gadget list and numbers:** not reachable. "Utilities" stays locked through Arena 2 (167 trophies), and no gadget slots show in the battle HUD at Arenas 1 and 2. Still open.

Other facts that update 02b:
- Ground is hilly in Arenas 1 and 2, not flat. Castles tilt on slopes.
- Round cap rises by arena: 12 in Arena 1, 14 in Arena 2.
- Losses still pay gold and wood.
- Daily chests: 5 a day, with an ad to reset.

## 6. What this means for Castle Protect
Ideas only. All names, art and rules stay our own.
- Keep pull-back aim with power and angle numbers. Make the aim line longer, or let upgrades extend it.
- No turn clock in single player, or an optional one. Pressure in these games comes from the online design, not from fun.
- If we copy nothing else, copy "aim low, break the base": collapse is the payoff.
- Give a readable miss marker (where the last shot landed). Pocket Tanks lacked it and it hurt.
- A light pick or draft before battle adds a real choice for little cost.
- Self-damage should be rare and clear, never a trap the AI sets that you cannot escape.
- Avoid a daily reward cap with an ad to reset. Avoid pushing the ad button above Continue.
- Let gadgets show up early so turns have variety from the first hour.
