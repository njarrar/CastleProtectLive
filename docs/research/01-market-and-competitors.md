# 01: Market and Competitors

Scope: artillery, castle siege and castle defense games. Written for Castle Protect, a single-player, offline, turn-based castle artillery game for iOS, Android and Web.

## How this was researched

- Web search across store pages, Wikipedia, review sites, Steam forums and app trackers. Done November 2026.
- Direct page fetches were blocked in this session (Google Play, App Store, Steam, Wikipedia did not resolve). All facts below come from search result text that quotes those pages. Numbers can lag the live store.
- Store numbers differ by region and date. Where sources disagree, both figures are given.
- "Unverified" marks any claim with only one weak source (fan wiki, AI summary site, mod APK site) or none.

## Game by game

### 1. Pocket Tanks (BlitWise)

- **Platform and year:** PC first, October 2001 after a beta. Later iOS, Android, Windows Phone, Microsoft Store.
- **Core mechanic:** Two tanks on destructible hills. Set angle and power, fire. Tanks can move a few steps per turn.
- **Turn model:** Strict turns. Each player picks weapons in a draft, then each fires one per turn until all are used. Highest score wins, not last tank standing.
- **Weapons:** 30 in the free game. Totals with Deluxe packs cited at 230, 320, 325, 385 (Wikipedia, Aug 2024) and 400 (App Store). Count grew over time.
- **Progression and meta:** None beyond unlocking packs. Every match is a fresh draft.
- **Monetization:** Free base game. Paid Deluxe upgrade and paid weapon packs. Some packs free with Deluxe. 15 mobile-only weapons need payment. No ads reported.
- **Rating:** Google Play about 4.34 from about 160K ratings.
- **Players love:** Long life, "never glitched and there has never been an ad", weapon variety, quick couch duels with a friend.
- **Players hate:** AI aim. "The AI hits with the first shot and every subsequent shot regardless of setting." Paid weapons break balance online. "Free" packs that need Deluxe. Asked for zoom out and a weapon search bar.
- **Take:** The weapon draft. Choosing 10 weapons from a pool before the match is a fast, deep decision with zero menus during play.
- **Avoid:** AI that never misses. Our AI needs human-like error that scales with difficulty.

### 2. Worms series (Team17)

- **Platform and year:** Worms 1995. Worms Armageddon 1999 (PC, consoles). Worms W.M.D 2016. Worms Rumble 2020. Worms Armageddon: Anniversary Edition, September 26, 2024 (PS4, PS5, Switch, Xbox, $24.99). A "Worms Across Worlds" 2025 entry appears on Wikipedia (details unverified).
- **Core mechanic:** Teams of worms on a destructible island. Each turn one worm can move, use a utility (rope, jetpack) and fire. Water kills.
- **Turn model:** Turn-based with a turn timer and a short retreat window after firing.
- **Weapons:** Armageddon has about 55 weapons. The 2D series has about 60 in total. W.M.D adds crafting, vehicles and enterable buildings.
- **Progression and meta:** Single-player missions and training. Little long-term progression. Unlocks via mission completion.
- **Monetization:** Premium.
- **Reception:** Armageddon: IGN 8.6, GameSpot 9.1. Fans call it the best entry. W.M.D: GameSpot 8/10, Metacritic "generally positive". Rumble (real-time): mixed. PC Gamer title: "Worms Rumble is fun, but it isn't Worms."
- **Players love:** Silly humor, wild weapons (sheep, banana bomb style), ninja rope mobility, buildings that add cover, crafting from map crates.
- **Players hate:** Rumble dropped destructible terrain and turns, and lacked progression. W.M.D: vehicles too easy to steal.
- **Take:** Utility items as weapons (rope, teleport, jetpack). Moving well is as fun as shooting well.
- **Avoid:** Leaving turn-based play. Rumble shows fans want the turn and the crater, not twitch shooting.

### 3. ShellShock Live (kChamp Games)

- **Platform and year:** Steam (early access then full release, about 2015). iOS version listed on the App Store.
- **Core mechanic:** Pocket Tanks style angle and power with tank movement, many map shapes, physics weapons.
- **Turn model:** Modes vary. Main mode is simultaneous turns (all aim, then all fire). Other modes are classic turns.
- **Weapons:** Counts differ: 200+ (early), 339 (2016 review), "over 250" (Dec 2025 review). Exact current count unverified.
- **Progression and meta:** Account level to 100, weapon XP, weapon upgrades, 80 single-player missions (early store text).
- **Monetization:** Premium on Steam, cosmetics.
- **Rating:** Steam "Very Positive", 93% of about 17K English reviews. SteamDB: about 32.7K reviews, 93.2% positive.
- **Players love:** Huge weapon list, unlock drip, campaign that "gives decent XP and lets you try new weapons", quick party games.
- **Players hate:** The grind. "Reaching level 100 probably takes about 1 to 1.5k hours." "You rarely unlock something new." Fun ends near level 80. XP-farm lobbies.
- **Take:** Weapon unlocks tied to play, plus weapon upgrades that change behavior, not only damage.
- **Avoid:** A long XP wall. Unlock pace must stay steady to the end of the campaign.

### 4. GunBound (Softnyx)

- **Platform and year:** PC online, from South Korea. 2003 launch per a weak source (unverified). Gravity Game Hub announced all servers close May 29, 2026.
- **Core mechanic:** Pick a "mobile" (vehicle class) with its own shot types. Angle, power and strong wind.
- **Turn model:** Delay system. Each action adds delay; lowest total delay goes next. Waiting adds 10 delay per second. Strong shots and items add more (Dual +600, Teleport +150).
- **Weapons:** Each mobile has a basic shot, a second shot and a special. Plus items.
- **Progression and meta:** Ranks, avatars with stats.
- **Monetization:** F2P. Avatars bought with gold or cash; cash avatars could boost stats a lot.
- **Players love:** Mobile variety, wind reading skill, delay strategy.
- **Players hate:** Pay to win. "You can buy your avatars, simply like bribing your way out to win."
- **Take:** The delay turn system. Big shots cost you tempo. This is a strong fit for a single-player tactics layer.
- **Avoid:** Stat boosts sold for money. Even offline, gear that trivializes play kills the fun.

### 5. Scorched Earth (Wendell Hicken)

- **Platform and year:** MS-DOS shareware, 1991.
- **Core mechanic:** Tanks on 2D terrain. Angle and power. Destructible ground, gravity, wind.
- **Turn model:** Turn-based, many players.
- **Weapons:** Large list including nukes, dirt weapons and diggers. Exact count not found.
- **Progression and meta:** Money per round. Buy weapons, shields, parachutes between rounds.
- **Monetization:** Shareware.
- **Reception:** Computer Gaming World 1993: "the most configurable artillery game I have come across."
- **Players love:** The shop and economy between rounds. Settings for gravity, wind, money, meteor showers.
- **Players hate:** Data not found (old game). Modern ports note slow turns with many AI players (unverified).
- **Take:** A between-round shop with defensive buys (shields, parachutes), not only guns.
- **Avoid:** Too many settings up front. Hide deep options behind a "custom" menu.

### 6. Tank Stars (Playgendary, later AI Games FZ / Azur Games listings)

- **Platform and year:** iOS, Android, Web portals. About 2018 (unverified). Developer name differs by listing.
- **Core mechanic:** 2D turn-based tank duel. Move, aim, fire. Each tank has several weapons.
- **Turn model:** Turn-based 1v1.
- **Weapons:** Many, such as stingers, spreads, rocket swarms, sinkholes, fireworks. Exact count not found. Tanks are near identical; weapons define them.
- **Progression and meta:** Weapon cards and coins from wins and chests. Each weapon upgrades to 5 stars. Tournament mode: two tanks vs waves.
- **Monetization:** F2P with ads, chests, premium tanks.
- **Rating:** Google Play 4.7 from about 2.86M reviews, 100M+ downloads. Sensor Tower 2024 US revenue snapshots are small (a few thousand dollars per week), so it is in a long tail phase.
- **Players love:** Fast duels, fun weapons, offline same-device 1v1.
- **Players hate:** "Core game content locked behind a paywall that doesn't even remove ads." Ads to claim win rewards. Paid tanks give an edge.
- **Take:** Tournament mode: a short gauntlet of waves with two units. Good template for a challenge mode.
- **Avoid:** Gating rewards behind ads. We are offline and should have no reward ads at all.

### 7. Castle Busters (listed as Castle Clashers!, com.epicoro.castleclashers, Voodoo)

- **Platform and year:** iOS (App Store id 6746328263) and Android. 2025 launch per listing dates. Owner's main reference.
- **Core mechanic:** You fight from inside a castle. You draft units into your keep. Each unit has its own attack. Win by killing all enemy units or reducing the castle to rubble. Walls, floors and towers break. You can blast firing lines and drop floors under enemy archers.
- **Turn model:** Conflicting. App Store copy says turn-based; Google Play says real-time 1v1. Unverified.
- **Units:** Squad of up to 8. Named types: knights, archers, bombers, siege engines, wreckers, napalmers, scouts, mages, fairy, crow.
- **Progression and meta:** "The Forge" upgrades units and castle. New arenas. Weekly leaderboard (mid-2025 update).
- **Monetization:** F2P, ads after matches, gold for castle upgrades, training timers on unit upgrades.
- **Rating / downloads:** Play about 3.4 to 3.5. Aggregator shows 10M+ and 4.6 (unverified). Needs network.
- **Players love:** The core idea: a castle you build and shoot from, plus floor collapse.
- **Players hate:** "After every game you play, you have to watch an ad." Ads "award nothing". "The grind now is a complete slog." Upgrade timers. Paying players "killing us with superior weaponry." Crashes in recent updates.
- **Take:** Units living inside a destructible castle. Hitting the floor under a unit is a better shot than hitting the unit.
- **Avoid:** Timers and gold walls on upgrades. Players saw the game get worse with each update.

### 8. Bowmasters (Playgendary, Miniclip listings)

- **Platform and year:** iOS August 2016, Android November 2016.
- **Core mechanic:** Two characters face off. Drag to set angle and power. Each character throws a different object and has a special.
- **Turn model:** Turn-based duel. Also bird and fruit shooting modes.
- **Characters:** 41 at launch, 60+ now.
- **Progression and meta:** Unlock characters with coins.
- **Monetization:** Banner ads, ads between matches, paid ad removal, a weekly subscription ($7.99/week, from a weak source, unverified).
- **Rating:** Play 4.2 to 4.3; AppBrain 4.53 from 6.1M ratings. 100M+ downloads.
- **Players love:** Ragdoll "fatalities", silly characters, dark humor, fast pass-and-play.
- **Players hate:** "Spent more time watching ads than actually playing." Repetition. Gore (Common Sense Media rates 14+).
- **Take:** Big, funny hit reactions. Every hit should look and sound great.
- **Avoid:** Gore as the joke. It narrows the audience. Use slapstick, not blood.

### 9. The Archers 2 (BYV) and archer duel games

- **Platform and year:** Android and iOS. About 2017 (unverified).
- **Core mechanic:** Stick figure archer duel. Pull back, aim the arc, release. Ragdoll hits.
- **Turn model:** Turn-based duel.
- **Weapons:** Bows, unlockable archers. Count not found.
- **Progression and meta:** Enemies aim better as you win more.
- **Monetization:** F2P, ads (typical of genre, details unverified).
- **Rating:** App Store 4.7 from 25K+.
- **Players love:** Simple controls, funny ragdoll, rising challenge.
- **Players hate:** Data not found in this pass.
- **Take:** Show the arrow arc only for a short way, so players learn the curve.
- **Avoid:** Endless same-looking duels. Needs map and enemy variety.

### 10. Angry Birds (Rovio, now Sega)

- **Platform and year:** Angry Birds 2009. Angry Birds 2 in 2015. Sega bought Rovio in 2023 for about $776M.
- **Core mechanic:** Slingshot birds at a static fort. Structures fall under physics. Each bird type has a tap ability (split, speed, bomb).
- **Turn model:** Single player vs a static target. A set number of shots.
- **Weapons:** Bird types. AB2 adds a card hand of birds and spells.
- **Progression and meta:** Stars per level. AB2 adds energy/lives, card choices, spells.
- **Monetization:** AB1 paid. AB2 F2P with lives and IAP.
- **Reach:** Rovio 5B+ downloads across titles. Angry Birds was the first mobile game to hit 1B downloads (Guinness).
- **Players love:** Chain collapses. Knowing a level has one perfect shot.
- **Players hate:** AB2 energy and lives. "A great game ruined by ridiculous microtransactions" (MobileSyrup).
- **Take:** 3-star scoring by shots left and damage. It drives replay of short levels.
- **Avoid:** Lives and energy. Never stop a player from replaying a level.

### 11. Crush the Castle (Armor Games)

- **Platform and year:** Flash 2009 (by Joey Betz, inspired by Castle Clout 2008). Legacy Collection on PC and mobile, March 2022.
- **Core mechanic:** Trebuchet. Time the release to fling stones at castles. Kill the royals inside.
- **Turn model:** Single player vs static target.
- **Weapons:** Boulders, split stones, exploding rocks, sticky bombs. Count not found.
- **Progression and meta:** Level packs. Score per level.
- **Monetization:** Mobile: free with ads, $4.99 one-time ad removal.
- **Rating:** Steam "Very Positive" (341 reviews).
- **Players love:** Satisfying collapses. Widely seen as the source of Angry Birds' formula.
- **Players hate:** Data thin. Low-confidence app score 3.05/5 from 19 users (unverified).
- **Take:** Targets inside the castle that must die, not just walls that must fall.
- **Avoid:** A timing-only launch. Pure release timing is hard to read on a phone.

### 12. Kingdom Rush series (Ironhide)

- **Platform and year:** 2011 browser, then mobile and Steam. Frontiers, Origins, Vengeance, Alliance (KR5), Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis launched September 24, 2026.
- **Core mechanic:** Tower defense on fixed paths. Four tower families with upgrade branches. A hero unit you move. Two active powers.
- **Turn model:** Real-time with pause.
- **Weapons / units:** Tower types with branches. Many heroes.
- **Progression and meta:** Stars per level fund a skill tree. Heroic and Iron challenge modes.
- **Monetization:** Premium, plus paid heroes. Vengeance locks about two thirds of heroes behind $3 to $8 IAP.
- **Reach:** KR1 100M+ downloads. KR6 Steam 91% positive (1,032 reviews).
- **Players love:** Polish, humor, clear tower roles, the star-driven upgrade tree, challenge modes.
- **Players hate:** Paid heroes in a paid game. Pocket Gamer on KR6: off-putting microtransactions in a premium game.
- **Take:** Stars feed a permanent upgrade tree. Replay old levels for stars, then spend them.
- **Avoid:** Selling power in a premium game. Keep the full roster earnable.

### 14. Stick War: Legacy (Max Games Studios)

## Category trends (2023 to 2026)

1. **Hybrid-casual growth.** Hybrid-casual was the only casual segment to grow in-app revenue in 2025 (reported +20% to $4.2B). Simple core plus meta progression. Castle Busters is a direct example.
2. **Ad fatigue.** Ad complaints top reviews for Castle Busters, Tank Stars, Bowmasters and Stick War. Reports say players want ads tied to real rewards, and studios test softer formats.
3. **Classic revivals.** Worms Armageddon Anniversary Edition (2024), Crush the Castle Legacy Collection (2022). Old artillery games still sell on nostalgia.
4. **Premium plus IAP backlash.** Kingdom Rush 6 (2026) scores well on Steam but critics fault paid heroes in a paid game.
5. **Old online games closing.** GunBound servers close May 2026. Online-only games die with their servers. An offline game does not.
6. **Offline demand.** "Offline Games - No Wifi Games" was named among the titles with the biggest rise in time spent in its genre (secondhand Sensor Tower summary, unverified detail).
7. **Destructible castles as the hook.** Castle Busters' 2025 updates added more destructible terrain and floor collapse. Destruction sells in short ads.
8. **Real-time drift fails for turn-based fans.** Worms Rumble went real-time and got mixed reviews. Turn-based remains the core of the artillery fan base.

## Gaps in the market

1. **No premium, offline, single-player castle artillery game with depth.** Pocket Tanks is offline but has no campaign or castle. Castle Busters has the castle but is online, PvP and full of timers.
2. **No good artillery AI.** Pocket Tanks AI is a sniper. Players want AI that misses like a person and has a personality.
3. **No artillery campaign with a real tech tree.** ShellShock Live has unlocks but a grind wall. Kingdom Rush has the star tree, but in tower defense.
4. **Castle building plus shooting in one loop.** Castle Busters hints at it. No one does "design your fort, then defend it in turn-based duels" offline.
5. **Fair monetization.** Every top mobile title gets dinged for ads, timers or paid power. A clean one-time price is rare in this space.
6. **Web plus mobile in one artillery game.** Browser artillery games exist but are thin ports. Few share saves across web and phone (would need export/import offline).
7. **Kid-safe slapstick.** Bowmasters is 14+ for gore. Room for a funny, bloodless artillery game for ages 9+.

## What a "best ever" single-player offline game here needs

1. **Readable, fair aim.** Angle and power with a short preview arc. Wind shown clearly. Undo-free, but a practice shot in early levels.
2. **50+ weapons with real behavior.** Bounce, split, dig, roll, build, freeze, teleport, shield. Each has one clear job.
3. **Destructible castle with units inside.** Walls, floors, supports. Units fall when floors break. Hits on structure matter as much as hits on units.
4. **A castle you build.** Pick blocks and room layout between missions. Then defend it.
5. **AI with personality and human error.** Named enemy commanders with styles (rusher, turtle, trickster). Error grows smaller with difficulty, never zero by default.
6. **A tempo system.** GunBound-style delay or action points: big weapons cost more time.
7. **Campaign of 60 to 100 levels** with stars, mission modifiers, bosses and world themes.
8. **A tech tree fed by stars and play,** with steady unlocks to the end. No XP wall.
9. **Replay modes:** daily challenge (seeded offline), gauntlet, sandbox, hot-seat optional.
10. **Huge feedback:** screen shake, slow motion on kills, comic hit reactions, bloodless.
11. **One-time price or free demo plus one unlock.** No ads, no timers, no energy, no paid power.
12. **Safe saves:** local, with export/import code for moving between web and phone.
13. **Short sessions:** a level in 2 to 5 minutes, pause anywhere.
14. **Accessibility:** colorblind-safe wind and trajectory, one-hand play, scalable text, aim assist toggle.

## Player types to target

1. **Nostalgic artillery fans (25 to 45).** Played Scorched Earth, Worms, Pocket Tanks, GunBound. Want depth, many weapons, no pay-to-win. Will pay once.
2. **Physics puzzle players.** Came from Angry Birds and Crush the Castle. Want the perfect shot and 3 stars.
3. **Casual mobile players tired of ads.** Play Tank Stars or Castle Busters but hate the ads and timers. Want offline play on commutes and flights.
4. **Builders and tinkerers.** Like base layout in Clash of Clans or Besiege. Want to design a fort and see it hold.
5. **Collectors and completionists.** Want every weapon, star and badge. Need a full collection screen and a clear finish line.
6. **Parents and kids (9+).** Want bloodless slapstick with no chat and no ads.

## Top 20 most fun mechanics in the genre (ranked)

1. **Destructible terrain and craters** (Scorched Earth, Worms, Pocket Tanks). Every shot changes the map.
2. **Angle and power aim with wind** (all artillery games). The core skill.
3. **Structural collapse with targets inside** (Angry Birds, Crush the Castle, Castle Busters).
4. **Wild, distinct weapons** (Worms, Pocket Tanks, ShellShock Live). Each weapon a new toy.
5. **Breaking the floor under a unit** (Castle Busters, Worms falls into water).
6. **Pre-match weapon draft** (Pocket Tanks).
7. **Mobility utilities: rope, teleport, jetpack** (Worms, Wormix).
8. **Split and mid-air abilities on tap** (Angry Birds bird abilities).
9. **Comic hit reactions and ragdoll** (Bowmasters, The Archers 2).
10. **3-star scoring per level** (Angry Birds, Kingdom Rush).
11. **Star-fed upgrade tree** (Kingdom Rush).
12. **Bounce and trick shots** (Hills of Steel bouncing shells, ShellShock Live).
13. **Between-round shop with shields and parachutes** (Scorched Earth).
14. **Tempo or delay turn order** (GunBound).
15. **Mission modifiers** (Stick War: "before sunset", "barricaded gold").
16. **Unit signature finisher or special** (Hills of Steel, Bowmasters, GunBound mobiles).
17. **Crates that drop on the map** (Worms W.M.D crafting crates).
18. **Enterable buildings as cover** (Worms W.M.D).
19. **Heroes placed on castle floors** (Grow Castle, Castle Busters).
