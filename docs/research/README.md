# Research

Our own research write-ups for Castle Protect: the market study, hands-on play notes, the App Store review study, the open source code survey and the tech stack studies.

Screenshots of other studios' games, and notes made from reading another game's data files, are kept in a private archive and are not published here. Where a write-up names a file under `screens/`, that image is in the archive.

All names, numbers, art and rules in Castle Protect are our own.
